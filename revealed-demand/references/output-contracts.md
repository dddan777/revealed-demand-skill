## D. Idea-stage response

Use when no meaningful behavioral evidence exists:

1. Testable demand claim.
2. Strongest existing signal and why it is insufficient.
3. Smallest commitment ask.
4. Manual or no-code delivery plan.
5. Observation window and threshold.
6. Stop or redesign rule.

Do not provide a precise optimal price. Provide a bounded price hypothesis and explain what the test is meant to learn.

## E. Sales-stage response

Use when transactions exist:

1. Normalize cohort, price, channel, and time window.
2. Separate gross sales, refunds, activation, repeat use, and repeat purchases.
3. Identify whether evidence is concentrated in one warm cohort.
4. Test the strongest rival explanation.
5. Check delivery economics and capacity.
6. Recommend scale only through a controlled expansion with a guardrail.
